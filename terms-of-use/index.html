2. Description of the App

The Captain Speaking App offers two main features:

  • Audio Exercises: Users can access over 500 small audio chunks of aviation communication with provided subtitles. Some key information is missed in subtitles, and after listening to each chunk, users are required to answer a quiz about what they heard.
  • ATC Communications Recordings: Users can listen to ATC (Air Traffic Control) communications in different cities. These recordings have been modified to remove silence from the audio.
